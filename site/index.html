    A little more about me.

    I’m a digital designer with a background in sales and marketing, a love for the little details, and a habit of asking a lot of questions.

    Somewhere along the way, the internet started to look the same. Templates, page builders, and AI make it easier than ever to launch a site, but so many end up feeling interchangeable. I miss the personality of the internet I grew up with. The unexpected details, the playful interactions, the feeling that someone made a space their own.

    I bring that curiosity to my work, alongside the practical questions of who it’s for and what it needs to do. I like figuring out how things work, finding better ways to do them, and making things that feel a little more human.
