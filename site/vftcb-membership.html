Valley Forge Tourism Membership Journey

The brief

Create a connected experience that helps businesses understand membership, get started, explore advertising opportunities, and renew.

What I did

As Membership Manager, I used my experience working with prospects and members to design the materials and digital experiences around each stage. I built the digital pieces with AI as my coding partner.

The result

Four connected projects covering the membership pitch, sign-up and welcome, co-op advertising, and renewal. Each responds to a different set of questions while carrying the membership story forward.

The challenge

Different stages bring different questions

A prospect wants to know how membership could help their business. A new member needs to understand what to do first. An existing member may be ready to explore advertising or decide whether to renew.

My role puts me in those conversations every day. I wanted the experience at each stage to answer the questions people bring to it and make their next step clear.

The approach

Design around the whole relationship

I mapped the journey from the first membership conversation through renewal, then designed the pieces around those moments. The pitch establishes the value, sign-up and welcome help members get started, the co-op shop introduces additional opportunities, and renewal brings the year’s work back into view.

Consistent language, benefit groups, and plan information connect the pieces. The visual treatment adapts to each purpose, including the annual report styling used for renewal.
