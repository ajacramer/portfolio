How we’ll work together

  1. 01Talk through your needs.

    We’ll go over who you’re presenting to, what you want them to do next, and the content you already have.

  2. 02Shape the story.

    I’ll organize your content into a clear flow and put together a design direction that fits your brand.

  3. 03Build it and try it out.

    You’ll click through it yourself on a laptop and a phone, and we’ll adjust anything that doesn’t feel right.

  4. 04Present it.

    I’ll make sure it runs smoothly where you’ll be presenting and show you how to share the link afterward.

A few things you might be wondering

Can you work from my existing PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Send me what you have and we’ll use it as the starting point for the content and the story.

Do I need special software to present it?

No. It runs in a web browser, so you can present it from a laptop and share it as a link.

Can I update it later?

We’ll talk about how often you expect things to change. I can set it up so text and numbers are easy to update, or handle updates for you.

Can you design a regular slide deck instead?

Yes. If an interactive version isn’t the right fit, I can design a PowerPoint or Google Slides deck.

How long does a presentation take?

It depends on the length and how much of the content is ready to go. Once we’ve talked through what you need, I can give you a timeline before we start.

Can we meet in person?

I’m based in Downingtown and work with businesses and organizations across Chester and Montgomery counties. If we can find a spot that works for both of us, I’m happy to meet over coffee. A video call works too.
