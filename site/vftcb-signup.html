Where it started

Signing up had to be quick

The presentation ends with a button to join. Whatever comes next has to keep that momentum: quick to finish on a phone, clear about price, and useful to our team the moment it arrives. Then, once someone joins, their listing needs to go live fast.

The idea

Same look as the presentation

The join and welcome forms share the presentation's night-sky look, so a prospect moves from pitch to sign-up without a change in style. The sign-up is five short steps with the offer always in view. The welcome page picks up right after, with the listing setup, the people they'll work with and a checklist of first steps.

  • Emails you can sort

    Each form sends our team a notification with a clear subject prefix, like **NEW MEMBER** or **LISTING INFO**, followed by the business name.

  • A staff-only converter

    A small tool turns a form export into an import file for Simpleview, our CRM. Drop in the export, pick the rows, download. It isn't linked anywhere and stays hidden from search.

  • Goals first

    The goals step asks which benefits matter most, so the team's first follow-up starts from what each business actually wants.
